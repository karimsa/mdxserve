Comments final mockup

The conversation timeline is the one thread layout, used in three places: the docked sidebar as the full view, a popover for reading a single thread in place, and the bottom sheet on a phone. Pick a highlight style at the bottom and every mockup updates. The reply fields are live.

Sidebar: the full view

Full height, flush to the window edge, on the page's own ground. The active thread sits on the card surface and its text is the darker highlight in the doc. Resolve is the checkmark beside each quote.

Popover: one thread, in place

With the sidebar closed, clicking a highlight opens its thread under the text with the same timeline. The three controls are resolve, open in sidebar, and close. Gutter counters show where the other threads are.

Phone and behaviour

  • Sidebar closed: clicking a highlight opens the popover.
  • Sidebar open: clicking a highlight focuses that thread in the sidebar; no popover.
  • Open in sidebar on the popover docks the panel with that thread active.
  • New comment: the selection button opens the popover with an empty thread and the field focused, whether or not the sidebar is open.
  • Escape or a click outside closes the popover. A typed, unsent reply is kept for that thread.
  • Phone: tapping a highlight raises the sheet scrolled to that thread; there is no popover and no gutter counter.
  • Resolved threads lose their highlight and move to the Resolved tab, where the checkmark becomes Reopen.
  • Read-only servers: same layouts without the field and the checkmark.

Highlight styles

Each sample shows a resting highlight, the active one, and how the style sits next to a link and inline code. All four use only a background and an underline, which is everything the browser's highlight feature can draw; rounded corners, padding and inline icons are not available.
